# Aurora — visual system

## Surface

Brand surface for a premium creator web presence. The complete route set is static and mobile-first. The visitor can explore Aurora’s selected photographs, follow an official destination, or prepare a local booking request.

## Art direction

Graphic, photographic fashion editorial: assertive first-person copy, sharp rectangular portraits, considered negative space, fine silver rules, a lipstick-red action, and one blush booking surface. The fixed page order and supplied copy are defined in the Aurora AI Builder package.

## Tokens

The canonical palette and typography are in `design/tokens.css`. Lacquer `#101114`, pearl `#F4F0EB`, lipstick `#A62B40`, and blush `#DEB8C5` carry the visual identity, with silver rules and muted text for hierarchy. Manrope Variable is self-hosted; no other display family is used.

## Composition

The Home page moves through six editorial sections: a split portrait opening, a light introduction, selected frames, a blush request invitation, official destinations, then a dark close. Booking is an understated pearl form with a factual dark aside. Gallery is a curated contact sheet with filters, progressive reveal, and an accessible viewer. Official links separate the creator page and wishlist from direct destinations. Booking Information, Privacy, and Exit are separate static routes. A native 18+ self-declaration precedes protected routes.

## Interaction

Booking stays in the browser: Details → Review → Ready to send. Values are not persisted or submitted to the website. Email, copy, and text download are visitor-controlled handoffs. Gallery reveals eight items at a time and supports filtering, keyboard navigation, touch swipe, and native video only when a published motion asset is assigned. Reduced-motion preference removes authored transitions.

## Boundaries

No server-side rendering, API, database, hosted form, booking provider, payment processing, analytics SDK, externally loaded font, invented service, rate, availability claim, testimonial, or confirmation state. A preview can run with a disabled email action and labelled missing-image placeholders. Production builds require the configured booking email, final domain, and required approved media.
