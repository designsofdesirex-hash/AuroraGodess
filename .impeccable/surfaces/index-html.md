## Scope and visitor mode

Brand surface, Persuade and Experience. Premium creator presence for Aurora; source of truth is the attached Aurora AI Builder package.

## Audience and job

Adult visitors arrive from Aurora’s social pages or direct shares. They can browse selected photographs, visit official destinations, and prepare a booking request to send manually by email.

## Action and proof

Primary action: request Aurora’s time through the native browser-only composer. Proof comes from approved local photography and exact official links.

## Constraints

- Plain static HTML/CSS/JavaScript; all seven routes work from generated static output.
- Exact copy, palette, Manrope, wordmark, route order, component behavior, and booking rules from the supplied builder package.
- Exact supplied X, Throne, Cash App, LoyalFans, and YouPay URLs.
- 18+ self-declaration is session-only and is not identity verification or access control.
- No backend, form submission, hosted service, payment integration, live availability, fake confirmation, analytics, or external font request.
- Use approved local media only; public build includes assigned derivatives only.
- USD only; no business rates, booking contact, domain, availability, claims, or response deadline may be invented.
- The request composer remains local until the visitor chooses email, clipboard, or text download.
- Studio lane: Designs of Desire, premium creator/local-business web presence.

## Chosen direction

Lacquer black, pearl, lipstick red, silver rules, and one restrained blush surface. Graphic fashion editorial with direct photography, square-cornered image frames, confident Manrope typography, and negative space. The home layout and every route follow the supplied desktop/mobile blueprints. No Bailey identity elements are retained.

## Open owner inputs

Real booking email, final site domain, approved service/rate data if any, and final photo/crop/alt approvals are held in the package’s configuration slots. Preview behavior is defined and may proceed with labelled missing slots.
